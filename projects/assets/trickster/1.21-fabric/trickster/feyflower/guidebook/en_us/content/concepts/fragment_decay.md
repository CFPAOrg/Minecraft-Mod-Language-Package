Some types of fragments, such as $$trickster:entity$$, have a rather loose connection to their targets.
While these fragments can generally be handled and used just fine, this imparts some restrictions that other types do not suffer from.


If at any point these so-called ephemeral fragments are inscribed, messaged, or otherwise transferred between spells,
they tend to decay into a unique fragment type referred to as $$trickster:zalgo$$.

;;;;;

This process makes them completely useless for their original purpose, 
but these $$trickster:zalgo$$ fragments do have a few unique properties that make them potentially useful in other ways.


To begin with, any one $$trickster:zalgo$$ fragment will never be equal to any other fragment, not even itself.


Additionally, feeding a $$trickster:zalgo$$ fragment into [Decaying Distortion](*trickster:tricks/distortions_stratagems/misc@trick&trickster:hash_values)
seems to produce a completely random and unique result every single time.
