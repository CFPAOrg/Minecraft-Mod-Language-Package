A wand is a very simple magical tool that will cast a spell inscribed into it when used with <feyflower:keybind id="key.use" /> or <feyflower:keybind id="key.attack" />, giving {#aa3355}True{} or {#aa3355}False{} respectively as first [argument](*trickster:tricks/delusions_ingresses/arguments).


Spells can be inscribed into a wand using [Notulist's Ploy](*trickster:tricks/basic#4).
