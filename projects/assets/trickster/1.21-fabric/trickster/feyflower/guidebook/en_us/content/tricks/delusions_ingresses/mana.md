Returns the amount of mana in the given storage.

;;;;;

#<trickster:trick id="trickster:get_max_mana_in_slot" />

;;;;;

#<trickster:trick id="trickster:get_mana_type_in_slot" />

Returns the types of mana in the given storage.

;;;;;

Gets the colors of mana in the given storage.
