Described here are [Scribing Patterns](*trickster:editing) which replace the glyph of the circle they're drawn in with a specific fragment.

;;;;;

#<trickster:revision id="trickster:two" />

Replaces the glyph with the number two. Other numbers may be created using [arithmetic](*trickster:tricks/distortions_stratagems/arithmetic).

;;;;;

#<trickster:revision id="trickster:empty_list" />

Replaces the glyph with an empty [list](*trickster:tricks/distortions_stratagems/list).

;;;;;

#<trickster:revision id="trickster:empty_map" />

Replaces the glyph with an empty [map](*trickster:tricks/distortions_stratagems/map).
