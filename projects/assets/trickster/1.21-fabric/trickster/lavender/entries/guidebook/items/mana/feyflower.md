DEAR SUN AND MOON ABOVE, THEY'RE FULL OF MANA!?


I hadn't paid much attention to these flowers before, but when my [Knots](^trickster:items/mana/knots) got close, 
they started refilling! At a rapid rate, too. Though... that rate appears to be decreasing. 
I don't think I should rely on them if I can avoid it.

;;;;;

To add to this discovery, upon closer inspection I can see that the flowers always droop leftward, 
no matter from which angle I look at them...


I wonder why they aren't mentioned in this book...


Regardless, they're pretty fragile. I *might* be able to move them with [Ploy of Exchange](^trickster:ploys/block#9), 
but I fear they may still be destroyed.

;;;;;

Their deceptive normalcy—despite their power—reminds me of tales of the fey. 
Because of this, I have decided I shall name them Feyflowers.


I just hope they aren't also as dangerous as the creatures in those stories...
