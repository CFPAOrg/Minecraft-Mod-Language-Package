A simple belt which can hold up to 4 [Knots](^trickster:items/mana/knots).


When worn around the waist, Knots attached to the belt may naturally recharge, 
and the mana within will be immediately available to the wearer.

;;;;;

<recipe;trickster:charging_belt>

To attach a Knot, simply right-click it onto the Belt.


Scrolling over the Belt will change the slot to prioritize, and right-clicking it again will detach a Knot.
