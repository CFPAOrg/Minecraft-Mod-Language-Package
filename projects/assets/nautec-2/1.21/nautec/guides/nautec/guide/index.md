# <Color id="gold">NauTec 2</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="aquatic_catalyst" scale="2"/>
  An ocean tech mod that runs on laser beams.
</Column>

NauTec machines run on laser power (AP) fired from one block into another. This guide covers making and routing beams, the machines that use them, breeding bacteria for resources, fitting augments to your body, and the Sea Scout submersible.

New to the mod? Read [Introduction](getting_started/introduction.md), then [Laser Power](getting_started/laser_power.md) to fire your first beam.
