# <Color id="light_purple">Cargo Hold Module</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="cargo_module" scale="2"/>
  Gives the Sea Scout a hold to carry loot home in.
</Column>

One Cargo Hold Module gives the hull a 27 slot hold, the size of a chest. A second one makes it 54, the size of a double chest. A third adds nothing.

***

## <Color id="gold">Opening the Hold</Color>

* While piloting, select the module on the bar and fire it (left-click or F). It costs no power and has no cooldown.
* From outside, stand behind the hull and right-click it. Right-clicking from the front or the sides still climbs in.

A Sea Scout cannot be stored in a hold, its own or another's.

***

## <Color id="gold">Keeping the Cargo</Color>

The cargo stays in the hull. Picking the hull up keeps it, and so does a hull that is wrecked and drops as an item, so placing it again gives you everything back.

Taking a Cargo Hold Module out keeps the cargo too, but you cannot reach it until the module goes back in. With two modules, taking one out hides the second half of the hold until you fit it again.
