# <Color id="light_purple">Deep Fauna Augments</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="abyssal_organ" scale="2"/>
  Three augments come from the creatures of the NauTec ocean biomes.
</Column>

These creatures always drop their part, and Looting can add one more. [Life in the Deep](nautec:getting_started/deep_life.md) covers where each one spawns. All three parts can also come out of lucky fishing zones (see [Fishing](nautec:getting_started/fishing.md)).

***

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="abyssal_organ"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Abyssal Eyes</Color>
</Row>

Part: Abyssal Organ, one from each Abyssal Maw. The Maw hunts in the Abyssal Trench below Y 40.

Slot: Eyes. Gives you night vision while you are at or below Y 45 (configurable, `abyssalEyesDepth`). Above that it does nothing, and the effect wears off within about ten seconds of rising past it.

It shares the Eyes slot with the <ItemLink id="guardian_eye"/>.

***

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="luminous_membrane"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Photophore Skin</Color>
</Row>

Part: Luminous Membrane, one or two from each Lantern Jelly in the Bioluminescent Grove.

Slot: Body. While you are in water, every living thing within 12 blocks of you glows, and the outline shows through blocks (configurable, `photophoreSkinRadius`). Out of the water it does nothing.

It takes the Body slot, the same one as flight and the Dolphin Fin.

***

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="chitin_plate"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Vent Carapace</Color>
</Row>

Part: four Chitin Plates, one in each of the four extensions. Each Vent Crawler in the Hydrothermal Vents drops one to three. Every extension holding a plate needs its own Claw Robot Arm and its own 25 AP beam (see [Augmentation Station](augmentation_station.md)).

Slot: Head or Body. Adds 4 armor and 50% knockback resistance, and fire burns you for half as long. Put it in the Head slot to keep the Body slot for something else. A second carapace in the other slot adds nothing.
