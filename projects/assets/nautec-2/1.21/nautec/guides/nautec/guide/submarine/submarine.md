# <Color id="light_purple">Sea Scout</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="submarine" scale="2"/>
  A two seat powered submersible.
</Column>

The Sea Scout carries you and one passenger through deep water on its own power cell, keeps you breathing while it has charge, and takes the hits instead of you.

It holds 1,000,000 power, has 80 hull points, and has nine slots for modules that add speed, weapons, sonar, shielding and teleporting.

***

## <Color id="gold">Launching</Color>

Hold the item and use it while looking at water within 6 blocks. From above the surface it launches where your view meets the water. If your head is already under water, it launches a few blocks in front of you.

The spot has to be water with room for the whole hull, which is large. If there is not enough open water you get "The Sea Scout needs open water to launch into" and keep the item. A breached hull will not launch at all until it is repaired (see [Hull and Repair](submarine_hull.md)).

***

## <Color id="gold">Seats</Color>

Right-click the hull to board. The first person aboard takes the front seat and pilots. The second sits behind and rides along, free to look about 85 degrees to either side, but only the pilot steers and fires modules. Two is the limit.

Sneak to climb out.

***

## <Color id="gold">Picking It Up</Color>

Sneak and right-click an empty hull to put it back in your inventory. The item keeps everything: stored power, hull damage, installed modules, module cooldowns and any name you gave it. Its tooltip shows the charge, the hull as a percentage and the installed modules. Everyone has to be out of it first.

In creative mode, hitting the hull picks it up straight away.
