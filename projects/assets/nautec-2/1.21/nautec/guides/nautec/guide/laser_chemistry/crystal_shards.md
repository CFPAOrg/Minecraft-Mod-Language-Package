# <Color id="light_purple">Prismarine Crystal Shards</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="prismarine_crystal_shard" scale="2"/>
  The best Aquatic Catalyst fuel, and a part in many advanced recipes.
</Column>

In an <ItemLink id="aquatic_catalyst"/> a shard gives the most power of any fuel, 20 AP per tick for 75 seconds at purity 1.2. Shards also go into the Prismatic Battery and other advanced recipes.

***

## <Color id="gold">From a Prismarine Crystal</Color>

A <ItemLink id="prismarine_crystal"/> gives up shards only to an [Aquarine Steel Pickaxe](tools.md) that is infused and has its ability on. Punch the crystal with it:

* Each punch puts one or two shards straight into your inventory.
* Each punch also has a 1 in 4 chance to shatter the crystal. If the pickaxe holds at least 100 AP when that happens, it spends 100 AP and the crystal drops three to seven more shards.

A shattered crystal is gone for good. If a crystal is powering a purity build, harvest a different one.

***

## <Color id="gold">From Budding Prismarine</Color>

<ItemLink id="budding_prismarine"/> grows clusters that break into shards, so this is the renewable source.

***

## <Color id="gold">From Prismarine Crystals</Color>

Drop <ItemLink id="minecraft:prismarine_crystals"/> into a laser beam of purity 2.0 or higher and each one becomes two shards after 8 seconds. A catalyst's own beam tops out at 1.2, so fire it into a Prismarine Crystal and use the beam that comes out, or pass a shard-fuelled catalyst beam through two Focusing Lenses in a row. See [Item Transformation](nautec:getting_started/item_transformation.md).
