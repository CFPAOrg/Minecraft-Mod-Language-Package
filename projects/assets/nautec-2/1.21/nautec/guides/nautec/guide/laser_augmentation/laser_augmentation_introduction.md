# <Color id="light_purple">Introduction to Laser Augmentation</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="eldritch_heart" scale="2"/>
  Augments are permanent upgrades built into your body at the Augmentation Station.
</Column>

An augment sits in one of your body slots and works on its own from then on, or on a key press for the active ones. No power is needed once it is installed, and augments stay with you when you die.

***

## <Color id="gold">What You Need</Color>

* An [Augmentation Station](augmentation_station.md): the station block, four Polished Prismarine, four Aquarine Steel Blocks and four <ItemLink id="augmentation_station_extension"/>, formed with the <ItemLink id="aquarine_steel_wrench"/>. Crafting the station takes an <ItemLink id="eldritch_heart"/>, so an Elder Guardian comes first.
* A <ItemLink id="claw_robot_arm"/> in the extension that holds the part.
* A laser beam of at least 25 AP entering that extension from below.
* The part itself.

***

## <Color id="gold">Where Parts Come From</Color>

* [Mob Augments](mob_augments.md): organs dropped by Guardians, Dolphins, Drowned and Elder Guardians.
* [Crafted Augments](crafted_augments.md): legs, arms, a flight tank and a spare heart chamber, made at the crafting table.
* [Deep Fauna Augments](deep_fauna_augments.md): parts from the creatures of the four NauTec ocean biomes.

JEI lists every part under Augmentation Effects, with the slots it fits and what it does.

***
