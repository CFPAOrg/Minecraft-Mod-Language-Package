# <Color id="light_purple">Booster Module</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="booster_module" scale="2"/>
  Ten seconds of extra thrust.
</Column>

Firing the Booster Module gives the thrusters 45% more push for 10 seconds.

***

## <Color id="gold">Using It</Color>

| | Default |
|---|---|
| Power per use | 20,000 |
| Boost | 45% more thrust |
| Runs for | 10 seconds |
| Cooldown | 13 seconds from firing |

Select it and fire it while the throttle is open. The boost adds to your thrust, so it stacks with holding sprint, and the hull leaves a bright trail from the stern while it runs. Its slot shows a bright strip until the boost ends.

The boost ends after 10 seconds, and the module is ready again 3 seconds later. At 20,000 a use it is cheap enough to fire freely on long crossings or to get clear of something.

Run it alongside the [Stealth Module](stealth_module.md) and the two multiply, leaving you about 23% faster than normal instead of slower.

All the figures are configurable.
