# <Color id="light_purple">Stealth Module</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="stealth_module" scale="2"/>
  Two minutes out of sight.
</Column>

Firing the Stealth Module hides the hull and its crew from creatures for two minutes, at the cost of some speed.

***

## <Color id="gold">Using It</Color>

| | Default |
|---|---|
| Power per use | 50,000 |
| Runs for | 2 minutes |
| Speed while running | 15% less thrust |
| Cooldown | 130 seconds from firing |

The moment you fire it, every creature within 32 blocks that was hunting the hull or anyone aboard gives up. For the next two minutes nothing can pick the hull or its crew as a target, however close it gets. Its slot shows a bright strip while stealth is running.

The hull gives up 15% of its thrust while stealthed. Fire the [Booster Module](booster_module.md) as well if you need to get away quickly.

When stealth ends, the module needs another 10 seconds before it can be fired again, and creatures can notice you as normal.

All the figures are configurable.
