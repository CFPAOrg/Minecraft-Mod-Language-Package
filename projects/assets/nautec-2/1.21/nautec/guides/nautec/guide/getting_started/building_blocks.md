</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="polished_prismarine"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Polished Prismarine</Color>
</Row>

The main NauTec building block. Beyond building, it goes into the Aquatic Catalyst, relays, most early machines and the Augmentation Station and Bio Reactor multiblocks.

  ### <Color id="aqua">Dark Prismarine Pillar</Color>
</Row>

A pillar that faces the way you place it, like a log. The [Bio Reactor](nautec:aquatic_biology/bio_reactor.md) uses it in its frame.

<Recipe id="nautec:dark_prismarine_pillar"/>

***

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="decorative_prismarine_crystal"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Decorative Prismarine Crystal</Color>
</Row>

A six block tall crystal that glows as brightly as a Sea Lantern. It is purely decorative.

It builds upward from the block you place it in and fills the five blocks above, so it only places where all six blocks are clear. Break any part of it to pick it up: the whole crystal comes down and drops as one item.
