# <Color id="light_purple">Aquarine Steel Armor</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="aquarine_steel_chestplate" scale="2"/>
  Aquarine Steel Armor runs on stored power and protects far better while charged.
</Column>

Each piece stores up to 512 AP. The power bar on the item shows its charge, and the tooltip gives the exact amount.

***

## <Color id="gold">Protection</Color>

Uncharged, the set gives 3 armor for the helmet, 8 for the chestplate, 6 for the leggings and 3 for the boots, plus 3 armor toughness and 10% knockback resistance per piece.

Each piece that holds power adds its own 10 armor and 5 armor toughness on top of that. Vanilla caps still apply (30 armor, 20 toughness), so a fully charged set sits at both caps. A piece that runs empty loses only its own bonus; the charged pieces keep theirs.

***

## <Color id="gold">Power Instead of Durability</Color>

The armor never loses durability. Damage that would wear it down drains its power instead, so a heavy fight empties it faster. Unbreaking cuts the power drain by 10% per level.

Charge it in a <ItemLink id="charger"/>. It does not need repairing.
