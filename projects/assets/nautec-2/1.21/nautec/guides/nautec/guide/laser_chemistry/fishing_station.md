# <Color id="light_purple">Fishing Station</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="fishing_station" scale="2"/>
  Fishes by itself and stores the catch.
</Column>

## <Color id="gold">Placement</Color>

Place it over open water. Every block in the 5x5 area under it, two blocks deep, has to be plain water. Kelp, seagrass, waterlogged blocks or Salt Water anywhere in that area stop it, so clear the area or pick a clean spot.

It checks the water every five seconds. The net turns while it is working.

***

## <Color id="gold">Power</Color>

Aim a beam into its top or bottom. Any amount of power runs it (1 AP per tick, configurable) and purity does not matter.

***

## <Color id="gold">Output</Color>

It rolls ordinary fishing loot once every two seconds (configurable) and stores the catch in 15 slots. Right-click it to open them and take the catch out, or pull it out of any face with a hopper or pipe. Once the slots are full, extra catch drops out of the station as items.
