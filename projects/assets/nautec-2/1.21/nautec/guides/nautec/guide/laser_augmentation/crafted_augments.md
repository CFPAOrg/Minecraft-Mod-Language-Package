# <Color id="light_purple">Crafted Augments</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="magnetic_coil_arm" scale="2"/>
  Twelve augment parts are made at the crafting table instead of dropped by mobs.
</Column>

Install each one at the [Augmentation Station](augmentation_station.md) like any other part.

***

## <Color id="gold">Legs</Color>

Four parts compete for the Left Leg and Right Leg slots.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hydraulic_leg"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hydraulic Leg</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Leap. Press Left Alt while standing on the ground to launch yourself in the direction you are looking. Look straight up for a vertical jump. It has a cooldown of just over a second.

<Recipe id="nautec:hydraulic_leg"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="servo_knee"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Servo Knee</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Step Up. You walk up full blocks without jumping.

<Recipe id="nautec:servo_knee"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shock_absorber"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Shock Absorber</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Prevent Fall Damage. You take no fall damage from any height. It pairs well with the Hydraulic Leg.

<Recipe id="nautec:shock_absorber"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="tendon_weave"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tendon Weave</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Walking Speed. Raises your base walking speed to two and a half times normal.

<Recipe id="nautec:tendon_weave"/>

***

## <Color id="gold">Utility Arms</Color>

Six parts compete for the Left Arm and Right Arm slots. These three work on their own.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="magnetic_coil_arm"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Magnetic Coil Arm</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Magnet. Pulls dropped items within 5 blocks toward you. Crouch to pause it, for example when you want to throw something away. To turn it off until you turn it back on, set a key for Toggle Magnet Augments in the Controls screen.

<Recipe id="nautec:magnetic_coil_arm"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ender_coil_arm"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ender Coil Arm</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Ender Magnet. Teleports dropped items within 5 blocks straight into your inventory. Items that do not fit stay where they are. Crouch to pause it, or use the Toggle Magnet Augments key to turn it off.

<Recipe id="nautec:ender_coil_arm"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hydro_drill_arm"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hydro Drill Arm</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Underwater Mining Speed. Removes the mining slowdown for having your head under water, the same as Aqua Affinity without the helmet. Mining while floating is still slow, so stand on the bottom.

<Recipe id="nautec:hydro_drill_arm"/>

***

## <Color id="gold">Throwing Arms</Color>

These three fire on a key press, each with a one second cooldown. They use no ammunition, and what they throw cannot be picked up.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="trident_launcher_arm"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Trident Launcher Arm</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Bouncing Trident, on Y. Throws a trident that ricochets once off the first block it hits and carries on. Hitting a mob ends the bounce.

<Recipe id="nautec:trident_launcher_arm"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="volley_trident_arm"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Volley Trident Arm</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Spreading Trident, on U. Throws a trident that splits into three when it hits a block, fanning out from the point of impact. Aim at the floor or a wall near a group of mobs.

<Recipe id="nautec:volley_trident_arm"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="syringe_robot_arm"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Syringe Robot Arm</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Random Potion Throwing, on G. Throws a splash potion chosen at random from Healing, Harming, Regeneration, Swiftness and Slowness. Two of the five hurt or slow whatever they hit, so do not throw it at your feet.

<Recipe id="nautec:syringe_robot_arm"/>

***

## <Color id="gold">Body and Heart</Color>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="buoyancy_tank"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Buoyancy Tank</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Creative Flight, in the Body slot. You can fly the way you do in creative mode: double-tap jump to take off, in water or out of it. It takes the Body slot, so it replaces a Dolphin Fin or Photophore Skin.

<Recipe id="nautec:buoyancy_tank"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auxiliary_ventricle"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Auxiliary Ventricle</Color>
</Row>

Augment: Bonus Hearts, in the Heart slot. Doubles your maximum health, from 10 hearts to 20. It shares the Heart slot with the <ItemLink id="eldritch_heart"/> augment, so you choose between more health and healing in water.

<Recipe id="nautec:auxiliary_ventricle"/>
