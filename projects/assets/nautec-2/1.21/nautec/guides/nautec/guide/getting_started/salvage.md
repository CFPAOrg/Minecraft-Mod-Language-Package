# <Color id="light_purple">Salvage</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="atlantic_gold_nugget" scale="2"/>
  Where NauTec's found materials and machine parts turn up.
</Column>

Many NauTec recipes need parts that you find and repair rather than craft. This page lists where they come from. [Ancient Machine Parts](machine_part.md) covers repairing them.

***

## <Color id="gold">Structures</Color>

| Where | What you find |
|---|---|
| Ocean Ruins (NauTec) | an Aquatic Catalyst, a Rusty Crate, Suspicious Gravel |
| Crystal Geodes | barrels, Crates and Rusty Crates full of parts and materials |
| Shipwreck treasure chests | a Crate two times in three |
| Vanilla ocean ruin chests | a Crate two times in three, and one time in four some loose salvage: Prismarine Crystal Shards, Atlantic Gold Nuggets, Rusty Gears, Burnt Coils, Ancient Valves, Damaged Aquatic Chips, Broken Whisks, Deep Kelp, and now and then a Prismarine Lens. The big ruins add a chance at Aquarine Steel Ingots, a Resonant Shard or a Diving Helmet |
| Warm ocean ruin Suspicious Sand | occasionally a Crate |
| Buried treasure | Damaged Aquatic Chips a little under half the time |

The Crates from vanilla chests hold Burnt Coils, <ItemLink id="brown_polymer"/>, Cast Iron Ingots, Aquarine Steel Compound, Atlantic Gold Nuggets, prismarine, iron and gold. See [Structures](structures.md) for the NauTec ones and [Crates and Etching](etching.md) for opening them.

***

## <Color id="gold">Mob Drops</Color>

| Mob | Extra drop |
|---|---|
| Guardian | one time in five: Ancient Valves, Burnt Coils or a Guardian Eye |
| Elder Guardian | always, three picks from Ancient Valves, Rusty Gears, Burnt Coils and the Eldritch Heart |
| Drowned | one time in five: a Broken Whisk or Drowned Lungs |
| Dolphin | one time in five: a Dolphin Fin |

The organs (Guardian Eye, Eldritch Heart, Drowned Lungs, Dolphin Fin) are augment parts: see [Mob Augments](nautec:laser_augmentation/mob_augments.md). The deep ocean creatures drop their own, covered in [Life in the Deep](deep_life.md).

***

## <Color id="gold">Fishing</Color>

Lucky fishing zones and the Prismatic Fishing Rod roll extra loot that includes Rusty Gears, Ancient Valves, Broken Whisks, Prismarine Lenses and Atlantic Gold Nuggets, plus rarer finds that depend on the biome. See [Fishing](fishing.md).

***

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="atlantic_gold_nugget"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Atlantic Gold</Color>
</Row>

<ItemLink id="atlantic_gold_nugget"/>s turn up in Crates, Rusty Crates and lucky zone treasure in any ocean. Nine nuggets craft an <ItemLink id="atlantic_gold_ingot"/>, and an ingot crafts back into nine nuggets. Later on you can make it reliably: an [Abyssal Pressure Forge](nautec:deep_engineering/pressure_forge.md) at Y -20 or lower presses a Block of Gold into two Atlantic Gold Ingots on a beam of purity 2.0, in 15 seconds.

The ingot goes into the Sea Scout, the Solar and Sonar Modules, the Auxiliary Ventricle augment and the Resonance Charm, and later into the Fusion Plant parts, the Crystal Cradle, the Prism Satellite and the resonance network blocks.

***

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="prismarine_lens"/>
  ### <Color id="aqua">Prismarine Lens</Color>
</Row>

A lens used in the Bacterial Analyzer, the Grafting Station, the Laser Injector, the Prismatic Emitter, the Impulse Laser Module, the Solar and Sonar Modules and several late machines. You can craft it from Aquarine Steel, and it also turns up as lucky zone treasure.
