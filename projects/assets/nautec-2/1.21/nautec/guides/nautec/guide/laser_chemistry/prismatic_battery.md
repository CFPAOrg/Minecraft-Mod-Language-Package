# <Color id="light_purple">Prismatic Battery</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="prismatic_battery" scale="2"/>
  Carries 10,000 AP and tops up the powered items in your inventory.
</Column>

## <Color id="gold">Using It</Color>

* Fill it on a [Charger](charger.md).
* Hold it and shift-right-click to switch it on. It glints while on, and the tooltip shows its status and charge.
* Put it in the Battery slot of your Curios inventory. It only works from that slot.

While it is on, every tick it moves up to 100 AP into each item in your hotbar and main inventory that stores AP and is not full, until the battery runs dry.

<Color id="gold">Tip</Color>: armour you are wearing is outside those slots. Charge worn armour on a Charger, or take it off for a moment.
